# AWJ User Guide

## Getting Started

### Initial Setup

1. **Install the App**
   - Install AWJ on your Android device (requires Android 8.0 or higher)
   - Launch the app from your app drawer

2. **Grant Permissions**
   - When prompted, grant **Usage Access** permission:
     - This allows AWJ to detect which apps you're using
     - Navigate to Settings → Special App Access → Usage Access
     - Find AWJ and enable it
   
   - Grant **Display over other apps** permission:
     - This allows AWJ to show the block screen
     - Navigate to Settings → Special App Access → Display over other apps
     - Find AWJ and enable it

### Adding Apps to Block

1. Open AWJ
2. Tap the **+** (plus) button in the bottom-right corner
3. Select apps you want to block from the list
4. Selected apps will appear in your blocked apps list
5. To remove an app, tap the "Remove" button next to it

### Setting Up Challenges

1. From the main screen, tap **View Challenges**
2. You'll see three default challenges:
   - Morning Exercise
   - Read Something Educational
   - Complete a Task
3. To add a custom challenge:
   - Tap the **+** button
   - Enter a title (e.g., "10 minute meditation")
   - Enter a description (e.g., "Meditate for 10 minutes")
   - Tap "Add"

## Using AWJ Daily

### Starting Protection

1. Make sure you have blocked apps and challenges set up
2. Tap **Start Monitoring** on the main screen
3. AWJ will now monitor your app usage in the background
4. A persistent notification shows that monitoring is active

### Completing Challenges

1. Throughout your day, complete your real-world challenges
2. Open AWJ and tap **View Challenges**
3. Check the checkbox next to each challenge you complete
4. Once ALL challenges are checked, the "Unlock Apps for Today" button becomes active

### Unlocking Apps

1. After completing all challenges, tap **Unlock Apps for Today**
2. Your blocked apps are now accessible for the rest of the day
3. The main screen will show "Apps unlocked for today! ✓"

### What Happens When You Try to Open a Blocked App?

If you try to open a blocked app before completing your challenges:
1. A red block screen appears
2. You'll see a message: "This app is blocked!"
3. Options:
   - **View Challenges**: Go to the challenges screen
   - **Go Back**: Return to your previous app

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Routine
1. Open AWJ
2. Review your daily challenges
3. Start monitoring if it's not already running

### Throughout the Day
1. Complete your challenges in the real world
2. Check them off in AWJ as you finish them
3. Once all complete, unlock your apps

### Next Day
- Apps automatically lock again at midnight
- Challenges reset to incomplete
- Repeat the process

## Tips for Success

### Setting Effective Challenges

1. **Be Specific**: "Do 20 push-ups" is better than "Exercise"
2. **Be Realistic**: Set challenges you can actually complete
3. **Start Small**: Begin with easy challenges and increase difficulty
4. **Mix Types**: Combine physical, mental, and productive tasks
5. **Morning First**: Consider challenges you can complete early in the day

### Recommended Challenges

**Physical Activity:**
- 10-minute walk
- 20 push-ups
- 15 minutes of yoga
- Climb stairs instead of using elevator

**Learning:**
- Read 10 pages of a book
- Watch one educational video
- Practice a language for 15 minutes
- Read a news article

**Productivity:**
- Make your bed
- Plan your day
- Clear email inbox
- Complete one task from to-do list
- Organize workspace

**Personal Development:**
- Journal for 10 minutes
- Meditate for 5 minutes
- Practice gratitude (list 3 things)
- Review and set goals

### Staying Motivated

1. **Track Your Progress**: Notice how productive you become
2. **Adjust Gradually**: Don't make it too hard too fast
3. **Celebrate Wins**: Acknowledge when you complete challenges
4. **Be Consistent**: Do it every day for best results
5. **Share Your Success**: Tell friends about your accomplishments

## Troubleshooting

### The Block Screen Doesn't Appear

- Check if you granted the "Display over other apps" permission
- Restart the monitoring service
- Ensure the app is in your blocked list

### Monitoring Stops Working

- Check if the app was force-closed
- Restart monitoring from the main screen
- Check if battery optimization is affecting AWJ
- Consider excluding AWJ from battery optimization

### Apps Stay Locked After Completing Challenges

- Ensure you tapped "Unlock Apps for Today" button
- Check that all challenges are marked as complete
- Try restarting AWJ

### I Want to Access a Blocked App Urgently

- Complete all your challenges quickly
- Or remove the app from the blocked list temporarily
- Remember: The goal is to build better habits!

## Privacy

- AWJ only monitors which apps are running
- No data is sent to external servers
- All information stays on your device
- No tracking, no ads, no data collection

## Best Practices

### For Best Results:

1. **Block Your Biggest Distractions**: Social media, games, etc.
2. **Set Morning Challenges**: Complete them early to unlock apps sooner
3. **Keep Monitoring Active**: Let it run all day
4. **Be Honest**: Only check off challenges you actually complete
5. **Review Weekly**: Adjust challenges and blocked apps as needed

### What Not to Do:

- Don't block essential apps (messaging, calls, maps)
- Don't set impossible challenges
- Don't cheat by removing apps temporarily
- Don't disable monitoring to avoid challenges
